Time,with teeth.

A free menu bar app for macOS that counts down to the trip, the launch, the birthday, or the next 25 minutes. Pin the ones that matter and they tick away next to your clock, and on your wrist.

Download for Mac Free. macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Halloween is a few weeks away. Somebody should be counting.

It lives in your menu bar.

No dock icon, no window to lose. Countdownula sits up top with your clock and only opens when you ask.

  1. Click the fang.

    Your countdowns drop down, soonest first, with a bar showing how much time has already drained away. Finished ones move to Past.

  2. Open one up.

    Each countdown gets a photo, a note, and its days, hours, minutes and seconds laid out big enough to feel.

  3. Pin what matters.

    A pinned countdown gets its own menu bar item: thumbnail, title and time left, always in view. Click it to jump straight to its details.

  4. Hear it hit zero.

    When a countdown finishes, macOS taps you on the shoulder with a notification. Timers work the same way, so a focus block ends on time.

On your wrist, too.

The Apple Watch app stands on its own, no iPhone needed. Check what's coming, open any countdown for a live view, or start a quick timer from your wrist. The watch sets its own alerts, so it taps you when time is up even if your Mac is asleep.

Each complication can follow a countdown you choose, or Next Up: your soonest pinned countdown, or the soonest one if nothing is pinned.

Your Mac and your watch stay in step through iCloud. Add a countdown on one and it shows up on the other, photo and all. It syncs through your own private iCloud storage, which only your Apple Account can read.

The Apple Watch app and iCloud sync arrive in Countdownula 1.1.

Invite it in.

Like any vampire, Countdownula has to be invited in. It isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you once before the first launch.

  1. Download the zip

    Get the latest Countdownula zip from the GitHub releases page and unzip it.

  2. Drag it to Applications

    Move Countdownula.app into your Applications folder so it sticks around.

  3. Open it the first time

    Right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again. Or run this in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Countdownula.app
