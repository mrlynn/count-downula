Privacy policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Countdownula doesn't collect your data. Your countdowns stay on your Mac. This website doesn't track you either.

This policy covers the Countdownula app for macOS and the website at www.countdownula.com. Both are made by Michael Lynn ("I", "me"). If anything here is unclear, email merlynn@gmail.com.

The app

Countdownula has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not make network requests, and I never receive anything you put into it.

  • Your countdowns. Titles, notes, dates, timers and photos are saved on your Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/Countdownula/. Photos you add are resized and stored in that folder. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Notifications. The app asks macOS for permission to show a notification when a countdown finishes. These are scheduled and delivered locally by macOS.
  • Backups. If you use Time Machine or another backup tool, your countdowns are backed up along with the rest of your files, under that tool's own terms.

If a future version adds syncing through iCloud, your countdowns would be stored in your own private iCloud database, which Apple operates under Apple's privacy policy. I would not have access to it. This policy will be updated before any such feature ships.

The website

The website is a set of static pages. It uses no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no embedded social media and no cookies. Fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties. The "try it" countdown runs entirely in your browser, and nothing you type into it is saved or sent.

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes basic request information, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver pages and protect against abuse. See Vercel's privacy policy for how they handle it. I don't use this information to identify or profile you.

The download link goes to GitHub, which has its own privacy statement.

When you email me

If you write to me for support, I'll receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. I use it only to reply and to fix what you reported. I don't add you to a mailing list, and I don't share or sell it. You can ask me to delete our correspondence at any time.

Children

Countdownula doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because I don't collect personal data through the app or website, there is nothing to access, export or delete on my side apart from any emails you've sent me. Your countdowns are under your control: delete the folder above and they're gone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Changes that affect how your data is handled will be noted in the app's release notes.
