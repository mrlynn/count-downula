Common questions

macOS says Countdownula can't be opened.

Count Downula isn't notarized by Apple yet, so Gatekeeper blocks the first launch. Right-click the app in Applications, choose Open, then choose Open again in the dialog. You only need to do this once.

You can also clear the quarantine flag in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Countdownula.app

Where did the window go?

Count Downula is a menu bar app, so it has no Dock icon and no main window. Look for the fanged timer near the clock at the top right of your screen and click it.

If your menu bar is crowded, macOS may hide items behind the notch. Quitting a few other menu bar apps, or holding Command and dragging icons to rearrange them, will bring it back into view.

I'm not getting a notification when a countdown ends.

Open System Settings, then Notifications, then Countdownula, and make sure Allow notifications is on. Focus modes such as Do Not Disturb can also hold notifications until later.

How do I pin a countdown to the menu bar?

Open a countdown from the list and choose Pin. It gets its own menu bar item with its photo, title and time left. Choose Unpin in the same place to remove it.

Where are my countdowns stored?

On each device, and in your private iCloud storage if you're signed in to iCloud. On a Mac the local copy is in ~/Library/Application Support/Countdownula/, and Time Machine backs it up with the rest of your Library.

With iCloud on, setting up a new Mac is just a matter of installing Count Downula and signing in to the same Apple Account. Countdowns from version 1.0 are brought across automatically the first time you open 1.1.

My countdowns aren't syncing.

Check that your Mac and watch are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud is turned on for Count Downula, and that there's free iCloud storage. Sync usually takes a few seconds, though photos can take longer on a slow connection.

iCloud sync needs Count Downula 1.1 or later on every device.

How do I get the Apple Watch app?

The watch app arrives with Count Downula 1.1 and needs watchOS 10 or later. It works on its own, without an iPhone app, and syncs with your Mac through iCloud.

To add a complication, touch and hold your watch face, choose Edit, swipe to the complications, and pick Countdownula. Each one can follow a countdown you choose, or Next Up.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. It's a universal app, so it runs natively on both Apple silicon and Intel. The Apple Watch app needs watchOS 10 or later.

How do I uninstall it?

Click the fang, choose Quit, then drag Countdownula from Applications to the Trash. To remove your countdowns from the Mac too, delete ~/Library/Application Support/Countdownula/.

If you used iCloud sync, a copy stays in your iCloud storage and on your watch. Delete it under Manage Storage in your iCloud settings.

Does it cost anything?

No. Count Downula is free, with no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.
