Cookie policy

Effective October 7, 2026

This website sets no cookies. There's no banner because there's nothing to consent to.

What this site stores

Nothing. www.countdownula.com doesn't set cookies, and it doesn't use local storage, session storage or any other way of saving data in your browser. There are no analytics, advertising or social media scripts, and fonts and images are served from this site rather than from third parties.

The live countdown on the home page runs in your browser's memory and disappears when you leave the page.

Other sites

Links to GitHub, such as the download button, take you to a site with its own cookie practices. See GitHub's cookie policy for details.

Our host, Vercel, doesn't set cookies on visitors to this site. Request information it processes to deliver pages is covered in the privacy policy.

The app

The Count Downula apps for macOS and Apple Watch are not web browsers and don't use cookies.

Changes

If this site ever starts using cookies, this page will be updated first, and you'll be asked for consent where the law requires it.
