Cookie policy

Effective October 9, 2026

This website sets no cookies. There's no banner because there's nothing to consent to. The live countdown pages at go.countdowncula.com don't set cookies either, but they keep a few keys in your browser's storage so you can edit what you've made there.

What this site stores

Nothing. www.countdowncula.com doesn't set cookies, and it doesn't use local storage, session storage or any other way of saving data in your browser. There are no analytics, advertising or social media scripts, and fonts and images are served from this site rather than from third parties.

The live countdown on the home page runs in your browser's memory and disappears when you leave the page.

Live countdown pages

The live countdown pages at go.countdowncula.com, and the countdown editor there, set no cookies and run no analytics, advertising or social media scripts. They use your browser's local storage only to keep keys you need, on that device:

  • the key that edits a countdown you made on the web, so you can change or delete it later
  • the key for a note or photo you left in a countdown's sealed coffin, so you can see or remove it before zero
  • the key for a guess you made in a date pool, so you can change it

These are strictly necessary for what you asked to do, aren't used to track you, and never leave your browser except to prove to the server that something is yours. Clearing your browser's site data removes them, and with them the ability to edit those things from that browser.

Other sites

Links to GitHub, such as the download button, take you to a site with its own cookie practices. See GitHub's cookie policy for details.

Our host, Vercel, doesn't set cookies on visitors to this site. Request information it processes to deliver pages is covered in the privacy policy.

The app

The Count Downcula apps for macOS, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Apple TV are not web browsers and don't use cookies.

Changes

If this site ever starts using cookies, this page will be updated first, and you'll be asked for consent where the law requires it.
