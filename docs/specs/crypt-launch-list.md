## Notes

- Sources disagree by a minute on some astronomical times (equinox 20:24 vs 20:25 UTC); the more precise figure is used.
- The Perseid peak varies by source (08:00–10:00 or 20:41 UTC on Aug 13), so it's listed as a local night.
- Super Bowl and Olympics times get set once announced; until then they'd count to the start of the day.
- Countdowns drop off a day after zero. Holidays roll to next year by adding next year's entry to `launch.json` and reseeding.
