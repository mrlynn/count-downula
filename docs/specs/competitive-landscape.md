# Countdown apps: what the others do

Status: research, October 9, 2026
Feeds: [phase 5](phase-5.md)

Sources are App Store and Play Store listings and product sites, checked October 9, 2026. "Not listed" means the listing doesn't mention it, not that it's confirmed missing. Many iPad apps show up on the Mac and Vision Pro only as "Designed for iPad", which isn't real Mac support. Links are at the bottom.

## The short version

Nobody else has Wallet passes, an App Clip, a sealed coffin, date pools, synchronized zero or a curated public feed. Those are ours.

The closest competitor is **Outside**: free shared countdowns, a browser link for people without the app, Home, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets, Live Activities, and a Pro subscription. Count Me In and Reach also do shared countdowns, with an invite code or a link.

Where we're behind is the ordinary stuff: repeats beyond yearly, Calendar and Contacts import, interactive widgets, Mac desktop widgets, and ways to count other than days and hours. Where nobody is strong is the big screen: Mac screensavers, Apple TV and full-screen modes are all abandoned or single-purpose.

## Table stakes we're missing

These show up in several competitors, and people will expect them:

- **Repeats beyond yearly.** Weekly, monthly and custom repeats are common.
- **Calendar import.** Countdowns (Shayes), Pretty Progress, Time Until and Outside (paid) all have it.
- **A generous free tier.** Several apps give unlimited countdowns free and charge for looks or sync. Our limit of 3 is stricter than most.
- **Interactive widgets and Control Center controls.** No competitor lists them yet, so getting there first is cheap.

## The big screen, which nobody owns

- **Mac screensavers.** The only one found is soffes/Countdown, open source and last updated about 7 years ago. No commercial countdown screensaver exists.
- **Mac menu bar.** Several small paid apps (Count Down, Days, CountdownBar, Motivation Timer), each showing one or a few countdowns. Ours already does more.
- **Apple TV.** A handful of single-purpose apps (Countdown: for TV, Timer TV, New Year's Countdown). None sync with a phone.
- **Full-screen and presentation modes.** Web tools can be shown full screen, but nothing is made for a TV at a party.

That's an opening. A New Year's Eve countdown on the living room TV, a launch countdown on the office screen, or a wedding countdown at the reception are the moments people film. They line up with synchronized zero and the crypt.

## Not found anywhere

Wallet passes, an App Clip, a maintained Mac screensaver, a tvOS app that syncs with a phone, a visionOS-native app, and Focus filters.

## Couldn't verify

Howlong and Hours Until (no listings found), Countdown+ (one blog mention), a Mac app called Vantage or Until (none found), the timeanddate web creator (page blocked), and all Android ratings and prices (Play Store pages didn't load). Dreamdays looks abandoned.
